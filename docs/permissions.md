# Permissions

Mississippi Valley State University (MVSU) and the Community College of Allegheny County (CCAC)
gave permission for their names and logos to appear in this project.

Their names and logos appear in the web app's university toggle, themes and views, and in
`playground/client_application/public/brand/` and `playground/client_application/brand-originals/`.
They are not covered by this repository's [MIT License](../LICENSE). If you reuse this code, remove
them or get your own permission first: see "Add a logo" in the [README](../README.md#customizing-the-gui).

Neither institution's data is in this project. The views show synthetic mock data from Achieving
the Dream, so nothing in them is a finding about MVSU, CCAC or their students.
